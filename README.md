Context: Andes Retail Group needed to evaluate the performance of its revenue, profit, margin, and sales volume, as well as identify performance differences across countries, product categories, customer segments, and time periods throughout the year. To this end, a data model and a dashboard were built, consisting of an executive summary view and a detailed analysis page.

Analysis: The analysis included KPIs for total revenue, profit, margin, and units sold, as well as visualizations of monthly trends, seasonality, revenue concentration by country, distribution by customer segment, and profitability by category. Filters and interactive elements were also incorporated to facilitate exploration of the results and comparison of performance across different areas of the business.

Conclusions: Andes Retail Group generated approximately $5.5 million in revenue and $1.94 million in profit, with a margin of nearly 35.1%. Peru and Chile accounted for a significant share of revenue, while the Premium and Standard segments represented the largest share of sales. Significant seasonal variations were also identified; therefore, it is recommended to prioritize the most profitable markets and segments, strengthen the categories with the highest margins, and adjust the sales strategy during periods of lower performance.
